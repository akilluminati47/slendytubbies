## Licence notes

* `CC Attribution` (CC-BY) — usable if you credit the author.
* `CC Attribution-NonCommercial` (CC-BY-NC) — credit **and** no commercial release.
* These are all rips/recreations of ZeoWorks' *Slendytubbies* games, themselves fan works on the
  *Teletubbies* IP (WildBrain). Treat this project as a non-commercial fan game; do not sell it.
* Every author used must be listed in an in-game CREDITS screen. `credits.md` is generated from
  `curated.json` by `tools/gen_credits.py`.
